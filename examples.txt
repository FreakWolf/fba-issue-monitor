BUCKET SELECTION EXAMPLES
================================================================================

The resolve modal bucket is determined by scanning keywords in the ticket:

Example 1:
  Text: "removal order damaged, unboxing video shows tampered box"
  Matched keywords: removal(Removal Damages), damaged(Removal Damages+Damages),
                    unboxing(Removal Damages)
  Selected Bucket: "Removal Damages" (3 matches)

Example 2:
  Text: "warehouse lost inventory, FNSKU reconciliation shows missing"
  Matched keywords: warehouse(Warehouse Lost), lost(Warehouse Lost),
                    inventory(Warehouse Lost), missing(Warehouse Lost),
                    reconciliation(Warehouse Lost)
  Selected Bucket: "Warehouse Lost" (5 matches)

Example 3:
  Text: "customer returned item in damaged condition, refund issued"
  Matched keywords: return(Damages), damaged(Damages), condition(Damages),
                    returned(Damages), refund(Damages), customer(Damages)
  Selected Bucket: "Damages" (6 matches)

Example 4:
  Text: "clawback deducted from account, reimbursement retracted"
  Matched keywords: clawback(Clawback Related Disputes),
                    reimbursement retracted(Clawback Related Disputes),
                    account(Clawback Related Disputes)
  Selected Bucket: "Clawback Related Disputes" (3 matches)

Example 5:
  Text: "item switched, received different item in removal"
  Matched keywords: item switched(Switcheroo), different item(Switcheroo),
                    removal(Switcheroo)
  Selected Bucket: "Switcheroo" (3 matches)

Example 6:
  Text: "FMV calculation is wrong, damage less reimbursed than fair market value"
  Matched keywords: fmv(Re-evaluation), fair market value(Re-evaluation),
                    damage less reimbursed(Re-evaluation)
  Selected Bucket: "Re-evaluation" (3 matches)

ASSIGNEE MAPPING SUMMARY
================================================================================

FBA Categories:
  Removals (all types)                    → nitheraj
  MFI                                     → vishancm
  Warehouse Lost/Damaged                  → harikks
  Order Related (all types)               → ankiwkum
  SAFE-T Claim Dispute                    → (no assignee, manual)

EF Channel (from SAFET Excel lookup):
  Channel = MFN                           → dhrubora
  INSPECTED_FORWARD_LEG_INSPECTION_FAILED → dhrubora
  INSPECTED_IMAGE_MISMATCH               → bamulyao
  SECURED_RETURN_RTS_IMAGES_MISMATCH     → snshekar
  SELLER_ABUSIVE_VERIFIED                → snshekar
  SELLER_ABUSIVE_VERIFIED_BLOCKED        → snshekar
  CTR (any pattern)                      → mansilko
  RNOTR_SELLER_REFUSED_DISPOSED          → khursheq
